Frequently Asked Questions

What is SelfMade?

Idle Business Tycoon: SelfMade is a free idle tycoon game for iOS and Android. You start with nothing, build businesses, invest in the stock market, buy real estate, and collect luxury lifestyle assets — private jets, superyachts, and private islands — as your passive income empire grows.

Is SelfMade free to play?

Yes. SelfMade is completely free to download and play. The core game — building businesses, investing, and acquiring lifestyle assets — requires no purchases. Advertisements are offered to players in exchange for rewards.

Does SelfMade work offline?

Yes. Your businesses and investments continue earning passive income even while you're not playing. Come back after hours or days and collect unfathomable amounts of income.

Is the money in SelfMade real?

No. Every dollar, investment, dividend and "passive income" figure in SelfMade is entirely fictional and has no real-world monetary value — SelfMade is designed purely for fun and fantasy.

Is SelfMade a cozy game?

Whether you're relaxing in a private jet cabin at cruising altitude or on the deck of your own yacht, SelfMade offers a low-pressure escape to a life without limits — while still giving players who want it real depth: a live stock market, a global rich list, and real strategic decisions along the way.

What kind of game is SelfMade?

SelfMade is an idle clicker game and incremental simulator in the tycoon genre. It combines business management, stock market trading, real estate investment, and a luxury lifestyle simulator into a single mobile game.

What can I do in SelfMade?

You can build and upgrade businesses across multiple industries, trade stocks and crypto on a live in-game market, invest in real estate properties around the world, buy luxury assets including private jets, superyachts, jewellery and fashion, and compete on a global rich list. The game features a full passive income system that grows even when you're not playing.

Is SelfMade similar to other idle tycoon games?

SelfMade shares the satisfying idle business-building loop of the tycoon genre, but goes significantly deeper — with a real-time stock market, real estate portfolio system, crypto trading, luxury asset collecting, and a rich list to climb. Where most idle games stop at a number going up, every business, property, car, yacht and jet in SelfMade comes with vivid photography and an in-depth description written like a page out of a private listing — the kind of detail you'd expect from a brochure handed to a billionaire, not a mobile game. It's built for players who want more depth, realism and atmosphere from the incremental genre.
