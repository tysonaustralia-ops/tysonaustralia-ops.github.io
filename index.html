About SelfMade

SelfMade is the idle game that lets you escape. Start with one small business and watch it grow into an empire, even while you are away. There is no one to answer to, just jets, yachts, mansions and a spot at the top of the Rich List.

Idle Business Tycoon: SelfMade is a free idle tycoon game for iOS and Android. It is an idle clicker and incremental simulator, so your businesses and investments keep earning while you are away. The core game needs no purchases, and ads are optional - only watch an ad if you want a reward.

Build and upgrade businesses, trade stocks and crypto on a live in-game market, invest in real estate around the world, and collect private jets, superyachts, jewellery and fashion. Every business, property, car, yacht and jet comes with vivid photography and a detailed description, written like a private listing.

All money in SelfMade is fictional and has no real-world value. It is designed purely for fun.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn money?

Start with one small business. Every business earns income every second, and you can upgrade it to earn more. Your empire keeps earning while the app is closed, and you collect the income when you return. Once you have the cash, you can invest in stocks, crypto and real estate.

What is the Rich List?

The Rich List is the global ranking of the wealthiest people in the world, and your goal is to reach number one. Your net worth is everything you own: your cash, your businesses and your investments, plus the properties, jets and yachts you collect.

What is the Family Office?

The Family Office is your own private team, with staff such as an estate manager, a chauffeur and a trainer. They send you notes about your homes, your cars and your daily life.

How do Sports Franchises work?

Buy a franchise in one of several sports and own your own team. Matches play out live, and your team's form affects the income it earns. Each season has standings and prizes.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads in SelfMade are optional - only watch an ad if you want a reward. There are no forced interruptions, so you can play the whole game without watching one.

What is Chairman Mode?

Chairman Mode is an optional subscription. It removes ads and gives you a Member Card and a badge.

Do I need to spend money?

No. SelfMade is free to play, and the core game needs no purchases. Optional purchases are available if you want to speed things up.
