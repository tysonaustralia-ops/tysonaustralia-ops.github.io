Privacy Policy

Cromdale Holdings Pty Ltd — Last updated 12 October 2026

Who we are

SelfMade (Idle Business Tycoon: SelfMade) is made by Cromdale Holdings Pty Ltd, Australia ("we", "us"). Address: 19 Beach Rd, Kingston Beach TAS 7050, Australia. Contact: admin@cromdaleholdings.com.

Who it is for

SelfMade is for players aged 16 or over. We do not ask for your date of birth. If you are under 16, please do not use the app. If we learn that someone under 16 has sent us information, we will delete it on request.

No accounts

There is no sign-in, no account and no cloud save run by us. Your progress and the optional avatar photo you choose are stored on your device. We cannot see them and cannot move them between iOS and Android. On iOS, deleting the app deletes them. On Android, if Android device backup is turned on, Google may keep a copy of your game save in your Google account backup and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new device; that copy is handled by Google under its own terms and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Ads

SelfMade shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch one. Active Chairman Mode members do not see ads.

When ads are used, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and device and ad-interaction information to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first through Google's consent form, and on iOS through Apple's tracking permission. You can change these choices at any time in Options (Ad privacy choices, where shown) and in your device settings. If you decline, you can still play; ads may be less relevant. Ads are restricted to content rated suitable for teens.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The Chairman Mode subscription and in-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details. We learn only which products you have bought or whether Chairman Mode is active, so the app can unlock them. Use Restore Purchases in the app if you change device.

What we do not do

We do not use analytics in the app. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or microphone. Google AdMob may estimate your general location from your IP address when it serves ads. We do not sell your data. The app makes no network requests of its own other than those made by Google AdMob and the Apple or Google store. The app keeps a short log of recent errors and app events on your device only; it is not sent to us.

Website

Our website, cromdaleholdings.com, may use analytics cookies only if you accept them in its cookie banner. Nothing is measured before you agree, and you can change your mind at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Australian Privacy Act or California law), you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, or withdraw consent. The only personal information we hold is what you choose to send us, for example if you email us for support. Email admin@cromdaleholdings.com to make a request. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator, in Australia the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Where data goes

Google (ads), Apple and Google (purchases) and our email provider may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and Australia, under their own safeguards.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date below. If the change matters, we will tell you in the app.
